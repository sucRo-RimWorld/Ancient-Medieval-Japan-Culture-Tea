## 2026-10-20 change-of-scope check — tea overconsumption and sleep only

**Gameplay gap / author decision:** low-joy historical tea remains outside drug/addiction policy. Repeated cups consumed close together alone may lower rest *gain during sleep*, recovering spontaneously; ordinary consumption gives neither an awake stimulant buff nor a penalty. The earlier zero-Rest/Hediff exclusion is superseded only for this one small, negative, excess-only behavior. Kogashi-yu is uncaffeinated and unchanged. No dependency, tolerance, withdrawal, stimulant euphoria or new need is authorized.

**Prior-art comparison re-evaluated against the same observed source and package boundaries:** Vanilla psychite tea supplies a psychoid drug rather than a historical leaf drink. Vanilla/MO has no matching non-drug tea with this narrow nonaddictive use case. VE Brewing Coffees and Teas 1.6 green tea is a DrugBase/Social drug with modern cooking machinery, buffs and unrelated products; it cannot meet the new rule as a standalone unmodified pack. SYR Coffee and Tea 1.6 explicitly has SyrCaffeine, addiction and rest/health outcomes and thus has substantially more mechanics than the desired conditional sleep-only downside. Erin's Hot Drinks describes nonaddictive tea but also positive consciousness effects, modern drinks and additional systems; **latest XML remains unavailable**, so no stronger claim of its exact 1.6 implementation is made. Existing AMJ Culture owns global drug policy and broad social Thoughts; neither it nor Grains owns tea's narrow consumption-side behavior. The minimal independent Culture Tea implementation remains justified; no other Mod added as a dependency or official compatibility target.

**Technical source review, not native test:** older decompiled RimWorld source `RimWorld/IngestibleProperties.cs` admits `outcomeDoers` on an ingestible irrespective of drug category; `IngestionOutcomeDoer_GiveHediff` adds severity; `Hediff.TryMergeWith` accumulates severity; `HediffCompProperties_SeverityPerDay` decays it; `Need_Rest.NeedInterval` multiplies sleeping gain by `StatDefOf.RestRateMultiplier`. The narrow nonaddiction XML Hediff with hidden subthreshold stage uses these Vanilla mechanisms, not borrowed Mod code. All of this remains **unverified on actual RimWorld 1.6**, and should not be represented as game acceptance.
