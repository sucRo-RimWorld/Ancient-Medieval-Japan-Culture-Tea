# Existing drinks alternatives audit (2026-10-20)

Scope: RimWorld 1.6, everyday historic tea plus kogashi-yu consumed as non-drug recreation.
Searches: RimWorld tea plants green tea drinks coffee mods, Workshop Erin's Hot Drinks, SYR Coffee and Tea, VE Brewing Coffees and Teas, existing AMJ Grains/MO.
Historical basis: Tea Docs/Research/PreOwnerStudy.md, including late-medieval kogashi and distinctions from Edo barley infusions.

1. Vanilla / Medieval Overhaul: Vanilla psychite tea is a psychoid DRUG and not ordinary Camellia tea. Vanilla RawRice is available for rice kogashi. MO provides equipment and grain but does not satisfy the pair of drinks. Existing MO 1.6 XML was inspected from supplied 3219596926.zip; no comparable combined everyday non-drug tea-and-kogashi drinks were identified. Do not claim exhaustive installed-runtime audit.
2. AMJ: Grains main Docs/Design.md (2026-10-20) owns rice and optionally AMJC_Barley and future EDIBLE kogashi, explicitly NOT drink finished goods. Culture owns drug behavior/Thoughts and Environment owns biomes. Tea is the only scoped drink owner.
3. VE: Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaBrewingExpanded-Coffees-and-Teas main commit 9e3cd65e56a3c707adf0b0d20c6f1e67e34a709c; directly inspected 1.6/Mods/VanillaCookingExpanded/Defs/ThingDefs_Items/Drinks_GreenTea.xml (DrugBase, drugCategory Social, CompProperties_Drug, added mood thought) and 1.6/Mods/VanillaCookingExpanded/Defs/RecipeDefs/Recipes_Tea_Cooking.xml (sugar, espresso machine, extra VCE dependency), plus About/About.xml (VBE, Harmony and VEF dependencies; coffee/tea catalogue). It has no documented medieval rice/barley kogashi. VE-content official compatibility is forbidden by Project policy. https://github.com/Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaBrewingExpanded-Coffees-and-Teas
4. Non-VE: emipa606/SYRCoffeeAndTea main bda24e238f931bb934f0b3c487437dabafe90f23. Inspected 1.6/Defs/Tea.xml: DrugBase, drugCategory Social, chemical SyrCaffeine, 0.03 addictiveness, health/rest/food/mood outcomes. 1.6/Defs/Recipes.xml: stoves and campfire can produce tea from leaves, but no dried-leaf intermediate or rice/barley kogashi; About/About.xml requires Harmony and declares beverage/drug policy additions; 1.6/Patches/CoffeeAndTea_Patches.xml patches unrelated Vanilla drinks and other Mods. https://github.com/emipa606/SYRCoffeeAndTea
5. Non-VE Erin's Hot Drinks Steam Workshop 2520989125 official description (checked 2026-10-20): non-addictive tea is present but has consciousness boost; coffee, chocolate, latte and mocha, and basic/modern beverage research are included. Workshop says save users should adjust drug policies; license CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. The actual latest XML and dependency tree were NOT retrieved. This is a material limitation of precise internals but not of the independent-use decision: the admitted other products and consciousness effect violate the approved narrow two-drink no-buff scope without hiding or rewriting a major portion of the mod. https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2520989125

Decision: implement two separate non-drug food/recreation items without optional VE content. Technical recipe/plant patterns may be studied but NO third-party art/XML is copied. This avoids unwanted drugs, extra content, compulsory Harmony/VE and duplicate food ownership. Independent scope and project prior-art audit complete for this narrow initial implementation only.
Limits: Non-VE Erin installed XML not available; RimWorld 1.6 native Def loading and repeated tea harvest still unverified; current API specifics require implementation and game validation. Avoid claiming tested outcomes. Other barley-provider compatibility is deferred until its exact ThingDef is inspected.
