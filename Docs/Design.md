## 2026-10-21 refined product principles — everyday tea without a drug system

**Stable decision:** Tea and kogashi-yu remain **non-drug everyday drinks**. The goal is historically plausible cultivation, preparation, consumption, and possible sale within a **normal mixed-background RimWorld colony**, not to recreate the institutions of an elite household or formal tea ceremony. An elite-organized starting scenario is not excluded, but a standard colony does not acquire elite tea ritual obligations from an individual's former status. Tea must not require Ideology, Culture, tea ceremony furniture, a ceremonial Pawn role, compulsory tea time, or a drug schedule. This deliberately favors the ordinary-colony loop while allowing people of any Backstory to drink tea.

**Medical versus gameplay classification:** Caffeine can cause real physiological tolerance and withdrawal even at comparatively low sustained doses (e.g. <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2262896/>), but physiological adaptation alone does not warrant treating ordinary tea as RimWorld chemical drugs, addiction, tolerance, or withdrawal. The user-approved omission is a **game abstraction**, not a medical claim that tea has no caffeine or cannot cause dependence. Conversely, do not invent exceptional cognitive/mood buffs to justify a drug category. Keep the existing **short-term excess-only sleep-recovery penalty** as the single modest negative caffeine effect. The current penalty's strength and threshold are **provisional gameplay values**, not a clinical caffeine limit.

**Serving/infusion interpretation:** In the current recipe, one finished `AMJT_BoiledTea` represents one serving prepared using a fresh recipe allocation of **two `AMJT_DriedTeaLeaves`**. The separate real-life concept of second, third or fourth infusions from the **same** leaves is **not implemented**. Three crafted servings therefore must not be explained as three successive infusions of one batch of leaves, nor converted directly to milligrams of caffeine using modern sencha concentration per 150 mL cup. If re-infusion is ever contemplated, account for diminishing extraction and added UI/labor burden before creating mechanics; no extra infusions, teaware, items or recipes are approved now.

**Scope of alternative tea:** Powdered/whisked tea is historically distinct from boiling leaf tea; its possible future ordinary consumption **does not imply tea ceremony or concentrated ceremonial koicha**. Neither powdered tea nor koicha is part of v1. If ordinary powdered tea is separately accepted later, it also stays non-drug and must not be used as a pretext for blanket ceremonial culture or separate addiction systems. Any specialized tea ritual belongs to a separately justified exceptional scenario/module, not automatic Culture Tea scope.

**Unit semantics and drying yield.** The same *six inventory units* pass from fresh to dried leaves, but they do **not** retain the same weight: six raw units weigh **60 g**, six dry units **12 g**, approximately **20% final mass**. This is a simplified water-loss conversion, **not** a claim that medieval methods used today's final tea processing. Modern Japanese MAFF first-flush harvest statistics report a crude-tea/raw-leaf weight ratio close to one fifth (2025 first flush: 20,000 t / 102,800 t), supporting the approximate physical scale; the boiling-leaf product and its technique remain historically distinct from modern sencha. Source: <https://www.maff.go.jp/j/tokei/kekka_gaiyou/sakumotu/sakkyou_kome/kougei/r7/tya/index.html>. The game quantity measures portions of raw and processed tea, not a 1:1 preservation of grams.

**Unapproved numerical illustration, NOT a fixed production loop.** Only **if** the provisional six-leaf harvest and six-leaf Bill were retained, one nominal shrub harvest would calculate as **6 fresh → 6 dry → 3 cups** (2 dry/cup). This relationship is an arithmetic consequence of guesses, **not evidence that the guess is the right yield or batch size**. First-stage drying requires **one Bill**, rather than leaving one fresh leaf from a 6-unit harvest waiting for a later 5-leaf batch; brewing three cups uses three existing Bills. Processing costs **250 + 3 × 180 = 790 work units** to convert all six leaves into three drinks. Alternatively, the player may keep/sell the dried stock without brewing. All yields are *nominal*; harvesting modifiers may lower actual yield, and the existing **6-day fresh shelf** remains as a forgiving buffer for mixed harvests and delayed batch processing rather than a historical claim of six days' optimal quality. The 6-input Bill must still be checked for small, low-yield colonies during native gameplay acceptance; avoid batch proliferation or speculative new workbenches before observing a problem.

**Brewed cup scale.** Two processed units are **4 g** of leaf to produce one portable **0.20 kg** prepared-drink item. Physical scale only: modern MAFF guidance is about 2–3 g per 100 mL for *sencha* (<https://www.maff.go.jp/j/seisan/tokusan/cha/attach/pdf/tea_export-7.pdf>); do **not** infer that medieval boiling tea is identical in brewing, extraction or caffeine yield. No 2nd–4th re-infusion from the same leaves is modeled; do not invent a caffeine dose in milligrams from inventory counts.

**Candidate-only arithmetic, not approved production economics.** If an unapproved six-leaf harvest is assumed, before trader discounts it represents **4.2 silver** as fresh leaves, **7.2** as six dried units (increment **3.0** for 250 work), or **9.0** as three brewed drinks (increment **1.8** for a further 540 work). This makes drying/transporting leaves the stronger trading loop and drinking the stronger colony-utility loop. `AMJT_KogashiYu` value **4.2** reflects three units of rice at 1.1 each (**3.3**, increment 0.9) or Grains barley at 1.2 each (**3.6**, increment 0.6); its former value **3.0** was below *both* ingredient valuations despite added processing. The displayed increments are gross market-value differences **before** planting/harvesting/processing time, fuel, transport, sale discounts, trader buy-filters or spoilage. Do not guarantee merchant demand, profitable exports or coffee/tea-specific traders.

**Intentionally unchanged balance:** fresh-leaf six-day rot, dry-leaf non-rotting storage and existing covered/outdoor deterioration, finished-drink two-day rot, `stackLimit`, both Joy values, nutrition (tea **0.01** technical, kogashi **0.15** from three × 0.05 edible grains), non-drug classification, excess-only caffeine `Hediff` and 3 separate Cooking Bills. The present cooking-work configuration favors modest production; player jobs, delays and nutrition UI are still subject to **one consolidated** later native acceptance. No cold tolerance, CCTO extension, shrub growth, fertility, temperature, research, art or merchant patch was changed.

## 2026-10-20 author-approved caffeine rule — excess tea only

**This is the current decision and supersedes conflicting v1 no-Hediff/no-Rest-effect exclusions below.** Tea remains **non-drug** (not a chemical, DrugCategory or DrugPolicy item), has no addiction, tolerance, dependency, withdrawal, caffeine Need, stimulant boosts or ordinary Rest restoration. However, repeated tea consumption within a short span may temporarily **lower sleep recovery efficiency**, not forcibly prevent sleep. Kogashi-yu has **no caffeine effect**. If powdered tea is implemented later, the same rule applies to its drink rather than a separate addiction system; powdered tea itself is not added by this change.

**Current XML-only source / explicitly provisional tuning (standard BodySize=1 pawn):** each cup of `AMJT_BoiledTea` adds 0.35 severity to temporary non-addictive `AMJT_TeaCaffeineLoad`. A hidden 0..0.9 stage has **no effect**; at 0.9 or more (usually **three cups within roughly 2–3 in-game hours**) a visible stage applies `RestRateMultiplier -0.15` during sleep, approximately 15% less rest gain at baseline. Severity naturally falls **1.4 per game day**, and Hediff disappears at zero. The penalty ends when severity falls below 0.9, even though the hidden harmless remainder persists for a short time. Maximum severity 1.4 prevents any additional escalating effect. No positive awake effects, mood penalty, medical disease or custom C# tick required. These are **actual current XML numbers but not play-balance acceptance**, not an assertion that this amount of historical tea was harmful. Evaluate excessive player-visible sleep penalties and unintended repeated recreational drinking before changing the values. Preserve a low-cost no-C# effect unless a demonstrated gameplay defect requires otherwise.

**Source balance correction (2026-10-21):** Kogashi-yu finished nutrition is now 0.15 rather than 0.05 (3 × 0.05 edible grains), while tea remains 0.01 and all Joy/caffeine, plant and trader numbers are unchanged. This is not a native test or acceptance.

**Game acceptance remains pending:** inspect actual 1.6 XML load, ingestion outcome + severity merge/decay, 1/2/3 servings with elapsed time, sleep recovery only above threshold, no drug handling and no penalty for kogashi-yu. No game-run PASS is claimed; API expectations below were corroborated from older decompiled code only, not the actual installed 1.6 assemblies.

Provide **exactly two modest everyday consumables**, `茶` (historically prepared tea) and `こがし湯` (grain flour toasted and stirred into hot water), as **non-drug** sources of small recreation. Preserve historical Japanese production without a drug/addiction system, dependency, withdrawal, positive stimulant/Rest buffs, strong mood boosts or emergency mental-break rescue. Repeated tea consumption alone has the narrowly approved transient sleep penalty described above.

This is a **Culture-series independent child Mod**, not an embedded module of Culture. Neither Grains, Culture, MO, Environment nor VE is a required runtime dependency. Do not add modern bottled/cold barley tea, coffee, black tea, matcha ceremonies, koicha, tea rooms, caffeine needs, new water need, addiction policy, medical/health buffs or extensive variant recipes. In ordinary colony play, accessible tea drinking takes precedence over elite ritual; exceptional scenarios retain their own freedom without expanding this Mod.

## 3. Historical boundaries

- Early Japanese tea includes boiling leaves; Song-derived powder/whisked tea appears later and coexists with boiling tea. Modern steeped sencha and standard tea-bag/cold tea are not automatically appropriate for the entire AMJ time span.
- Historical `こがし` includes **roasted grain powders involving rice and barley**; the 1587 Kitano tea-gathering allowed `こがし` as a substitute for tea. This is not proof that all cereal grains were interchangeable.
- **Kogashi-yu is consumed with the actual flour**, unlike later barley-kernel extract `麦湯` or modern clear `麦茶`. Those later drinks are **not** v1 products.
- Early tea's circulation differs by century, class and region. The 1256 Daigoji record concerns temple distribution in a limited setting, **not** universal everyday rural consumption (<https://company.books-yagi.co.jp/archives/8929>). Conversely, the **1403 Toji one-cup-for-one-coin tea seller** is a concrete example of medieval **commercial tea accessible to visitors**, not proof of ubiquitous nationwide home tea drinking (<https://khirin.rekihaku.ac.jp/pid/nmjh_collection/H-318.html>; <https://hyakugo.pref.kyoto.lg.jp/?p=118>). A player's tea-growing colony is a feasible playstyle **abstraction**, not an assertion that all periods and regions grew and consumed tea in the same way. No global wild tea spawning without ecology/historical review.

## 4. Gameplay and integration contract

- Manual right-click drinking and ordinary **voluntary** recreational ingestion should work. Prefer Vanilla Def/Recipe/Joy pathways before C#/Harmony/novel Jobs; do not require DrugPolicy, chemical-drug comps or a custom need.
- Ordinary hunger/job selection must **not** treat tea or kogashi-yu as staple meals. Nevertheless, kogashi-yu needs a **positive** nutrition value consistent with swallowed grain; a technical micro-nutrition experiment is not an accepted final balance value.
- Both give **small, saturating recreation**, with no addiction/withdrawal, chemical drug category, strong Thoughts, Rest restoration, working-speed buffs or built-in food-poisoning risk. The **sole exception to the old no-Hediff rule** is the hidden cumulative tea status becoming a short sleep-rest penalty after overconsumption; it is not a drug or addiction. No per-pawn caffeine tracking across days, drug policy scheduling, scheduled tea ceremony or new class-specific Tea Thoughts.
- Tea plant remains after harvesting and can be harvested again, subject to growth/work. Use existing plant mechanisms where feasible; do not confer magic blight immunity. A perennial plantation needs not just a tile-yield comparison but cumulative **planting, reharvesting and processing labor**, seasonal viability and a genuine opportunity cost against food agriculture.
- Use existing low-tech fire/cooking benches where viable; no mandatory special tea building, MO mill or research just for this module. Preparations/finished drinks should be distinguishable in stockpiles and UI. Dried tea is storable, finished drinks should not become indefinite stores. Do not add a drying rack, separate tea-making job type or work-category overhaul merely to represent one small processing chain. If cooking labor becomes a *measured* obstacle to tea-growing gameplay, first consider an appropriately batched **existing** recipe, not mandatory new facilities.
- **Trade identity and gate:** `AMJT_DriedTeaLeaves`, not cooked cups or perishable fresh leaves, is the intended **transportable tea commodity**. The current `MarketValue=1.2` per unit is only a provisional item statistic, **not evidence that vanilla or MO traders will accept it** or that players can profit. Examine actual sellability and representative Vanilla/MO trader `StockGenerator` category filters, then evaluate sale proceeds net of field and processing work. Prefer narrow owned item/category compatibility; do not force every trader to buy tea, automatically create a merchant overhaul, or inflate market value before those checks. Trade and peddler-system expansion remains the Project's optional unowned idea.
- **Culture** owns general `AteWithoutTable` interpretation. Tea must not copy its global Thought patch. Verify standalone eating/drinking behavior independently.
- **Environment** owns habitat/biome changes; optional natural tea distribution requires evidence and separate alignment.
- Compatibility with unrelated drinks Mods is not guaranteed. Preserve normal coexistence unless a demonstrated conflict justifies a narrow patch.
