# Ancient & Medieval Japan — Culture Tea / 中世日本 - 文化・茶

A standalone **Culture-family child Mod** (optional even without AMJ Culture) for historically grounded Japanese **tea** and **kogashi-yu** (toasted grain-flour drink). Both provide modest recreation without drug, addiction or strong medicinal effects.

- **Tea:** grow a renewable tea shrub, heat/dry leaves, prepare historical boiled tea.
- **Kogashi-yu:** roast and grind grain into flour, mix it with hot water. Vanilla rice is the base ingredient; optional AMJ Grains barley joins the **same** recipe. Because flour is swallowed, kogashi-yu provides a little nutrition.
- Edible kogashi belongs to [AMJ Grains](https://github.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Grains); modern barley tea is not included.

**Repository renamed 2026-10-20:** `Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Culture-Tea`. Display name and pre-release packageId match the Culture child-Mod family; the unchanged `AMJT_*` Def identities are not rewritten. Culture core and Grains remain optional.

**Status: development XML exists (unverified).** No validated game build or Steam publication yet. Recipe/joy/nutrition numbers are provisional, graphics use temporary Vanilla placeholders, and actual loaded-game behavior remains unverified.
